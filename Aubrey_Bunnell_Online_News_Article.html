Fall Farmers Market Brings New Flavors to Fountain Hills

Saturday market gives the community another way to shop local

FOUNTAIN HILLS, Ariz. - The Fall Fountain Hills Farmers Market is bringing local food and seasonal flavors to the community every Saturday in September. The market is open from 8:00 to 11:30 a.m. at Tractor Supply Co., located at 17130 E. Shea Boulevard.

More than 20 food vendors are taking part in the market. Shoppers can find fall-inspired items featuring flavors such as pumpkin, apple, maple, caramel, cinnamon, and sweet potato. Several familiar vendors from the town's Wednesday farmers market are also participating.

Marketgoers are encouraged to arrive early.
Town of Fountain Hills

The final Saturday market on September 26 will also include a Kids Market. More than 30 young entrepreneurs between the ages of five and 16 are expected to sell handmade crafts, artwork, food, games, and services. The event gives young people a chance to practice creativity, communication, and business skills.

The fall market is important because it supports local sellers while giving Fountain Hills residents a fun community activity. Visitors are encouraged to bring reusable bags, and overflow parking will be available across the street near Monterey Boulevard.

Event Details

When: Saturdays in September, from 8:00 to 11:30 a.m.

Where: Tractor Supply Co., 17130 E. Shea Boulevard, Fountain Hills, Arizona

Information source: Town of Fountain Hills
