Meet the designer

Hi, I’m Aubrey.

I’m building Websites by Aubrey around a simple idea: a website can have personality and still be easy to use.

A creative approach to the web

I enjoy bringing colors, layouts, and ideas together. My pink checkerprint design is playful and recognizable, while the pages stay organized so the information is easy to find.

For this student portfolio, I’m putting my HTML and CSS skills into practice through a website for my own design business. The sample work shows directions I can explore as I keep learning.

What matters in my designs

  • Clear headings and comfortable reading space
  • Layouts that adapt to phones and larger screens
  • Descriptive links and useful image descriptions
  • A consistent style from the first page to the last

A good website starts with listening.

Your audience, goals, and content guide the design.
