The steps in detail

  1. Discover

    Identify the audience and the main goal. Collect the business story, services, and style preferences.

  2. Plan

    Organize the pages and decide what goes where.

    • Outline navigation and page headings.
    • Gather text, images, and image descriptions.

  3. Create

    Build layouts that adapt to different screens and apply the brand colors consistently.

  4. Review

    Check the experience before publishing.

    • Test navigation and keyboard controls.
    • Review spelling, image descriptions, and contrast.
    • Check pages on a phone and a larger display.
